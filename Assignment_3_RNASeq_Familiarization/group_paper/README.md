## Short summary of the experimental design ##

The study acclimated *Epinephelus coioides* fish before dividing them into groups under normoxic (CM, DO 6.0 mg/L) and hypoxic (H, DO 0.6 mg/L for 8 h) conditions. Surviving fish were further classified into EMS (tolerant) and EMW (sensitive) groups according to their response to hypoxia. Muscle tissues were collected from each group, followed by RNA extraction, library preparation, and paired-end sequencing. This design allowed researchers to compare gene expression between control, tolerant, and sensitive fish to understand hypoxia adaptation mechanisms.
